Welcome!

Thank you for your interest in the Sports Nut Swim Teacher Academy. We're looking for enthusiastic people who want to develop leadership skills, gain real work experience and perhaps become the next generation of Sports Nut swimming teachers. The Academy is aimed at students in Years 11–13, but older applicants who are new to swimming teaching are very welcome to apply too.

How the Academy works

1. January 2027: shadow our experienced teachers for 4 weeks to see if teaching is for you. After that, you're paid while you keep learning.

2. April 2027: take the STA Level 2 Swimming Teacher course. We pay half the course fee.

3. September 2027: teach your own classes with Sports Nut.
